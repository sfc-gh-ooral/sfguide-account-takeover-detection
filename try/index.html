Where the false positives come from

  • Takeover sequence also fires for customers who change their phone and pay a new contractor the same day.
  • Impossible travel fires for corporate VPNs, and when a traveller logs in again after landing.
  • New device + country fires for travellers who buy a local phone.
  • Unusual amount fires for one-off large purchases.

No single rule is enough; the score rewards combinations, such as a credential change followed by a new payee from a new device abroad.
